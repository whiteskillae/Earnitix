# ✍️ Earnetix Content & SEO Strategy

## 1. Brand Identity
*   **Vision:** "Earn Through Content. Grow with Skills."
*   **Mission:** To help users earn in a structured way while building real, marketable digital skills.
*   **Aesthetics:** Modern, minimal, clean UI with high readability.
*   **Voice:** Motivational, youth-focused, and transparent.

### 🔡 Typography
*   **Primary Font:** `Inter` or `Outfit` (Modern Sans-serif).
*   **Heading Font:** `Lexend` or `Sora` (Clean, geometric for readability).

## 2. SEO Keywords (Target Audience: India/Global Youth)
*   **Primary:** "Earn money online with skills", "Skill-based earning platform", "Learn and earn ecosystem".
*   **Secondary:** "Task-based income", "Online earning tutorials", "Structured earning for creators", "Earn money by completing tasks".

## 3. Page Structure Requirements
*   **Home:** Vision, core features, and strong "Start Learning" CTA.
*   **How it Works:** Step-by-step breakdown (Learn -> Pay -> Earn).
*   **Benefits:** Focus on skill growth, trust, and structured income.
*   **Tutorials Preview:** Teaser content to encourage conversion.

## 4. Technical SEO Checklist
*   **Headings:** Strict hierarchy (H1 for page titles, H2 for sections).
*   **Metadata:** Unique titles and descriptions for all 10+ public pages.
*   **Schema:** `FAQPage` schema for the FAQ section; `SoftwareApplication` schema for the platform.
*   **Social:** Open Graph tags for WhatsApp/Instagram sharing.

## 5. FAQs (Optimized for Trust)
1.  **Is Earnetix a random earning app?**
    *   No. We are a skill-based platform where you learn through tutorials and perform structured work to earn.
2.  **Why do I need to pay for tutorials?**
    *   To ensure you receive high-quality, professional guidance and to maintain a secure, premium ecosystem.
3.  **Is my payment secure?**
    *   Yes, we use Cashfree, one of India's leading secure payment gateways.
4.  **Can I download the tutorials?**
    *   No. Content is streamed securely to prevent piracy and protect intellectual property.
