1. What is Earnetix?
Earnetix is a creator‑driven earning platform where users generate income by completing digital tasks such as:

- Creating reels  
- Writing blogs  
- Sharing promotional content  
- Engaging in affiliate-style promotions  
- Completing brand‑based creator tasks  

Unlike typical earning apps where users watch videos, Earnetix focuses on content creation, not passive viewing.

Earnetix is similar in concept to other online earning platforms that pay users for completing tasks and content activities , but your model is creator-first, not viewer-first.

2. How Earnetix Works
Earnetix operates as a task marketplace for creators. Brands, campaigns, or the Earnetix team publish tasks, and creators complete them to earn money.

Workflow
1. User signs up and gets a creator dashboard.  
2. Tasks appear based on the user’s category (Reel Creator, Blogger, Promoter).  
3. User completes the task (e.g., create a reel, write a blog).  
4. Earnetix verifies the work for originality and quality.  
5. Payment is added to the user’s Earnetix wallet.  
6. User withdraws earnings once they reach the minimum payout.

This model is similar to platforms that pay users for completing tasks such as writing articles or creating content .
